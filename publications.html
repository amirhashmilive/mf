Sustainable River Basin Management and Rural Hydrology

Authors: Dr. A. K. Sharma, Amir Hashmi, Prof. Robert Chen

Abstract: This paper presents an empirical analysis of community-assisted riverbank afforestation and seasonal water level monitoring across central Indian tributaries. The findings highlight a 24% reduction in soil erosion along restored banks.
