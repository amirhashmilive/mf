01 · Overview

Hand the community the microphone

A testimonial without a name is a rumour. Every voice below is attributed to a named individual, with a verifiable role and location. We do not write testimonials for people — we transcribe what they tell us, and we publish only what they sign off on.

Named, not anonymised

Every testimonial carries a real name, role, and location. No 'a farmer from Dhamtari said…' — only 'Lakshmi Dhruv, SHG secretary, Mohdi village.'

Signed off by the speaker

Before publication, each testimonial is returned to the speaker in their own language. They edit, approve, or withdraw. Withdrawn testimonials are not replaced silently.

Five-star verified

Each testimonial carries a five-star rating — not of Meer Foundation, but of the speaker's experience of the specific programme they participated in.

How a testimonial is made

A field officer conducts a recorded interview in the speaker's preferred language (Hindi, Chhattisgarhi, or English). The recording is transcribed and translated. The transcript is returned to the speaker — who edits, approves, or withdraws. Only approved transcripts appear on this page. The original recording is archived for verification, accessible to journalists and researchers on request.

02 · Eight testimonials

From eight voices, in their own words

Each card below carries the speaker's quote, name, role, location, programme badge, and a five-star rating of their experience. Quotes are verbatim from approved transcripts — edited only for length, with ellipses shown.

03 · Awareness

Why named testimonials matter

An anonymous testimonial is not a testimonial. These slides explain the principles that govern how we collect, verify, and publish first-person voices from the field.

Testimonial principles
8
Named, not anonymised
Voices

Every testimonial carries a real name, role, and location. No 'a farmer said…' — only 'Lakshmi Dhruv, SHG secretary, Mohdi village.'

Consent, then quote

Consent is recorded before the interview, and re-confirmed after transcription. The speaker can withdraw at any time, even after publication.

Verifiable location

Each testimonial carries a village or town name. Journalists and researchers can request a field visit through the press desk.

Verbatim, edited only for length

Quotes are verbatim from approved transcripts. Ellipses mark deletions. We do not paraphrase, even when the original is grammatically imperfect.
