01 · Overview

Counted, not estimated

The Meer Foundation impact ledger is built bottom-up. Every household is counted, every SHG is named, every kilometre walked is mapped. The numbers below are not extrapolations — they are aggregations of village-level records that any skeptical observer is welcome to inspect.

Bottom-up counting

Household counts begin at the SHG register, are aggregated at the village level, then at the cluster, then at the district. No top-down estimation, no extrapolation.

Skeptical Observer Standard

Before publication, each figure is audited: could a skeptical observer re-derive it from documented sources? If not, the number is revised or withheld.

Refreshed every six months

The ledger is updated each April and October. Numbers that are no longer source-verified are removed, not silently rounded.

Reading the ledger

The six primary counters below animate as they enter your viewport — each represents a category of impact that has been continuously verified for 14 years. The secondary grid shows newer or narrower figures. The SDG alignment map connects each number to the relevant UN Sustainable Development Goal.
