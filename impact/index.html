01 · Overview

Real-time change, result-oriented initiatives.

Meer Foundation's impact is not a marketing slogan. It is a five-page ledger: statistics, case studies, district profiles, project gallery, and testimonials. Each page documents a different facet of the same reality — rural Chhattisgarh is changing, and we are walking alongside it.

Skeptical Observer Standard

Every figure on these pages is audited against an internal standard that assumes a skeptical observer will check the source. If we cannot show the village list, we do not show the number.

Worked with, not for

We do not intervene in villages; we are invited. Every household counted is a household that consented to be counted. Every SHG formed is an SHG that named itself.

Five Pillars Framework

All impact is organised across our practice areas — Environment, Education, Livelihood, Health, and Women's Empowerment — and verified through the Five Pillars Framework (A-E-I-O-M).

03 · Awareness

Why documentation matters

An impact number without a source is a rumour. These awareness slides walk you through the principles that govern how Meer Foundation counts, walks, listens, and reports.

Impact principles
1L+
Counted, not estimated
Households

Every household figure is built bottom-up from SHG registers and village surveys — never extrapolated from a sample. If the village ledger does not add up, the number is revised.

Audited numbers

Every figure in our impact ledger is audited against the Skeptical Observer Standard before publication.

Verified outcomes

Outcomes — not outputs — are what we count. Bridges built matter less than crossings made.

Community attested

Before a number is published, the village it came from signs off on it. Community attestation is the final filter.
