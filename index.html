River Conservation Flagship

Bolti Nadi — Giving Voice to Chhattisgarh's Rivers

Bolti Nadi ("Speaking River") is Meer Foundation’s flagship river conservation initiative. By combining participatory hydrological mapping, community river stewards, and seasonal waterbody rejuvenation, we bring dying tributaries back to perennial life.

Institutional Standard

The Five Pillars AEIOM Architecture

A verifiable governance and impact reporting model ensuring transparent, evidence-based, attributable NGO operations.

A

Accountability

Answerable for pre-specified outcomes and public commitments across all field programs.

E

Execution

Operational field discipline converting intent into measurable rural reality.

I

Initiative

Originating from grass-roots community needs, not top-down administrative mandates.

O

Outcome

Demonstrable, attributable change — measuring long-term community uplift.

M

Measurement

Rigorous empirical data collection, field audits, and transparent public reporting.
