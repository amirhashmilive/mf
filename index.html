River Conservation Flagship

Bolti Nadi — Giving Voice to Chhattisgarh's Rivers

Bolti Nadi ("Speaking River") is Meer Foundation’s river restoration initiative. By combining participatory hydrological mapping, community river stewards, and seasonal waterbody rejuvenation, we bring dying tributaries back to life.

Institutional Standard

The Five Pillars AEIOM Architecture

A institutional communication framework for transparent, evidence-based, verifiable NGO operations.

A

Accountability

Answerable for pre-specified outcomes and public commitments across all programs.

E

Execution

Operational field discipline converting intent into measurable rural reality.

I

Initiative

Originating from grass-roots community needs, not top-down administrative mandates.

O

Outcome

Demonstrable, attributable change — measuring actual community uplift.

M

Measurement

Rigorous empirical data collection, field audits, and transparent reporting.

IJMEER Journal & Empirical Field Studies

Meer Foundation publishes peer-reviewed research through the International Journal of Multidisciplinary Educational and Empirical Research (IJMEER). Our studies cover river hydrology, rural pedagogy, CSR impact verification, and community resilience.
