01 · Overview

A scholarly initiative of Meer Foundation

IJMEER is Meer Foundation's contribution to the global scholarly ecosystem — an open-access, peer-reviewed journal that welcomes research across 70+ disciplines. The journal operates with full editorial independence and its own dedicated website at www.ijmeer.com.

Open access

Diamond Open Access — free to read, free to publish. All articles under CC-BY 4.0. No paywalls, no embargoes.

Multidisciplinary

70+ disciplines spanning engineering, social sciences, law, environmental research, public health, heritage, and climate science.

DOI-assigned

Every article receives a registered DOI through Crossref. Citations are persistent, indexed, and discoverable.

This page is a brief showcase. For the full editorial board, archives, author guidelines, peer review policy, publication fees, submission portal, and all journal-related queries, please visit the journal's dedicated website.

02 · Highlights

Key features

What makes IJMEER distinctive in the South Asian scholarly publishing landscape.

Peer-reviewed

Double-blind peer review with transparent post-publication reviewer attribution. COPE guidelines followed.

Regular publication

Published by Meer Foundation with a regular publication schedule. Visit the journal website for the current issue calendar.

International board

15 editorial board members from India, the USA, Türkiye, the UK, and Indonesia. Full board details on the journal website.

Diamond Open Access

Free to read, free to publish. No article processing charge for read access. Visit journal website for the full APC policy.

Bilingual abstracts

Abstracts published in English and Hindi. Full-text translations supported for high-impact articles.

DOI & indexing

Every article receives a DOI registered with Crossref. Indexed in Google Scholar. DOAJ application planned.
