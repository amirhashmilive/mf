What is an ISSN?

An International Standard Serial Number (ISSN) is an 8-digit code used to identify serial publications such as scholarly journals, magazines, and periodicals globally.

It provides standard recognition for indexing databases, libraries, and academic citation repositories worldwide.

Benefits of ISSN Registration

  • Global indexability in Google Scholar, DOAJ, and Scopus.
  • Standard identifier for researchers, libraries, and universities.
  • Enhanced credibility and copyright protection for published papers.
  • Unambiguous citation tracking across academic databases.
