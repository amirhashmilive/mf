About Meer Foundation

Driving Excellence in Research, River Conservation, and Rural Empowerment since 2011.

Our Mission

To enable sustainable rural development, revive local water bodies through community river conservation, and champion multidisciplinary research and open education.

Our Vision

A world where rural communities thrive with self-sustaining ecological practices, accessible literacy, and global academic exchange.

What Guides Us

Core Values

Empowerment

Fostering grassroot leadership and self-sufficiency in rural areas.

Sustainability

Protecting rivers and ecosystems through long-term environmental stewardship.

Academic Integrity

Rigorous peer-reviewed publishing standards and open science.

Inclusivity

Creating opportunities regardless of background, gender, or geography.
