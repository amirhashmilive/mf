01 · Overview

The education practice area

Education is the foundation of every other practice area. Without quality learning, there is no climate action, no health awareness, no rural enterprise, no river restoration. That is why Meer Foundation's education initiatives span every age group — from Class 1 to certification-level professionals.

Our mission

Bridge the rural-urban learning divide in Chhattisgarh. Provide CGBSE-aligned study materials, climate-science certification, and platforms for rural children's voices.

Our approach

Materials first, devices second. Every PDF works offline. Every course is mentor-led. Every magazine is authored by the students themselves — not for them.

Our impact

128 PDFs, 2,847 students, 3 distinct initiatives, 4 free awareness courses. 82% Prayaas Bastar pass rate in CGBSE Class 8 — up from a 41% baseline.

02 · Three initiatives

Three initiatives, one mission

The education practice area is delivered through three distinct initiatives, each addressing a different gap: school-level materials (Prayaas), professional certification (EduSuTo), and student-authored expression (Guriya Magazine).
