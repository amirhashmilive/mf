Walk Itineraries

Discover Chhattisgarh's Sacred Heritage

Immersive guided explorations led by local historians and indigenous elders.

Ancient Water Architecture

Exploring historical stepwells (baolis), temple ponds, and earthen check-dams demonstrating centuries of indigenous water engineering.

Sacred Grove Sanctuaries

Walking through ancient community Devgudis — biodiversity hotspots preserved for millennia by tribal customary laws.

Living Artisan Guilds

Direct engagement with master Dhokra bronze casters, terracotta artisans, and Godna tattoo artists preserving rare tribal arts.

Conservation

Community Preservation Funds

Walk registration contributions directly fund the physical restoration and seasonal cleaning of historic water heritage structures.

Education

Student Field Immersions

School and university batches gain firsthand understanding of sustainable architecture, oral histories, and rural ecology.
