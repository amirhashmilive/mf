Guriya — a magazine by rural children

A quarterly children's magazine authored and illustrated by rural and tribal school students of Chhattisgarh. Published in Chhattisgarhi, Hindi, and English.

4Quality Education10Reduced Inequalities
Bilingual

Chhattisgarhi · Hindi · English — all in every issue.

Quarterly

Four issues per year. Free for rural schools.

Children-authored

80% of content written and illustrated by children.

Curriculum-aligned

Tied to CGBSE language and art curricula.

Submit your work. Children from any Chhattisgarh school can submit stories, poems, or art to Guriya. Selected contributors receive a printed copy and a certificate.

Free for schools. Bulk print copies are distributed free to rural primary and middle schools in Dhamtari, Kanker, Bastar, and Balodabazar.
