Health-emergency awareness

Critical emergency awareness materials for rural Chhattisgarh. All materials available in English + Hindi, designed for ASHA workers, SHG members, schoolteachers, and family caregivers.

Cardiac emergency

Emergency
Beginner · 15 min read

Heart attack recognition and first response. CPR basics. When to call 108. Aspirin protocol.

Stroke awareness

Emergency
Beginner · 15 min read

Recognising stroke with the F.A.S.T. test. Golden-hour principle. What NOT to do.

Respiratory distress

Emergency
Beginner · 15 min read

Asthma, pneumonia, choking, COPD. When breathing becomes an emergency. Inhaler use.

Diabetic emergency

Emergency
Beginner · 15 min read

Low sugar vs high sugar. When to give sugar. When NOT to give anything. Insulin safety.

Allergic reactions

Emergency
Beginner · 15 min read

From mild rash to life-threatening anaphylaxis. EpiPen use. Common triggers.

Trauma & injuries

Emergency
Beginner · 15 min read

Bleeding, fractures, burns, head injury, snake bite. Correct first response.

Mental health crisis

Emergency
Intermediate · 20 min read

Panic attacks, suicidal thoughts, severe agitation, psychosis. Kiran helpline 1800-599-0019.

First aid quick reference

Emergency
All Levels · 10 min read

The 10 most common rural emergencies. Print this. Stick it on your wall.

These materials save lives. Print and distribute at SHG meetings, schools, and anganwadi centres. All PDFs are open-access. No registration required.
