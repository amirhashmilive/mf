Health Architecture

Three Pillars of Village Wellness

Integrating ancient Ayurvedic health wisdom with modern clinical screening.

Daily Village Yoga Sanghams

Morning community yoga and pranayama sessions on village grounds, fostering mental clarity, physical endurance, and communal harmony.

Maternal Care & Nutrition

Nutritional counseling for pregnant and lactating mothers, hemoglobin monitoring, and distribution of iron and folic acid supplements.

Adolescent Health Literacy

Demystifying menstrual health, personal hygiene, and reproductive wellness for teenage girls in remote tribal schools.

Preventive First

35% Drop in Emergency Visits

Villages practicing daily Yoga Kutumb routines experience significantly fewer preventable respiratory and gastrointestinal ailments.

Women-Led

Trained Village Yoginis

Over 120 rural women have been certified as local yoga instructors, earning respect and leadership roles within their Gram Sabhas.
