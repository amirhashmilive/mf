01 · Overview

What is EduSuTo?

EduSuTo (Education for a Sustainable Tomorrow) is Meer Foundation's educational initiative designed to build practical knowledge and skills in sustainability, climate action, and future-oriented development. It bridges academic learning with real-world application.

Our mission

Build capacity in climate science, carbon management, and sustainable development for students, professionals, and grassroots practitioners across India.

Our approach

Combine academic rigour with field-based learning. Every course includes real-world case studies, mentor support, and a capstone project.

Our impact

180+ alumni across 3 cohorts. 12 CCS capstone projects. 4 free awareness courses reaching 2,000+ rural practitioners.

Outcome guarantee. Complete the capstone to receive a verifiable certificate. If your project does not pass panel review, you may re-attempt once at no extra cost.

04 · Learning Modules

Detailed module breakdown

Each module includes learning objectives, key concepts, downloadable materials, and a practice quiz. Click a module card to expand.

Foundations of Climate Science
Week 1–2 · 12 hours
Learning objectives
  • Earth's climate system. Atmosphere, hydrosphere, cryosphere, biosphere, lithosphere; radiative forcing and feedback loops.
  • Greenhouse gases & radiative balance. CO2, CH4, N2O, fluorinated gases; GWP; IPCC AR6 carbon budgets.
  • Observational evidence. Temperature records, ice cores, sea level rise, extreme weather attribution.
Carbon Capture Technologies
Week 3–5 · 18 hours
Learning objectives
  • Post-combustion capture. Amine scrubbing, membrane separation, solid sorbents; energy penalty.
  • Pre-combustion & oxy-fuel. Gasification, water-gas shift, oxy-combustion cycles.
  • Direct Air Capture (DAC). Liquid solvent and solid sorbent DAC; energy and cost trajectories.
Transport & Storage
Week 6–7 · 12 hours
Learning objectives
  • Pipeline & ship transport. Compressed CO2 logistics; phase behaviour; network design.
  • Geological storage. Saline aquifers, depleted oil & gas fields, basalt mineralisation.
  • MRV. Geophysical, geochemical, atmospheric monitoring; blockchain-enabled systems.
Cluster-Based Project Development
Week 8–9 · 16 hours
Learning objectives
  • Industrial cluster mapping. Identifying emission sources, shared infrastructure, capture-ready opportunities.
  • Techno-economic modelling. CAPEX, OPEX, LCOE, carbon abatement cost; sensitivity analysis.
  • Stakeholder & policy design. Permitting, community engagement, carbon credits, government schemes.
Capstone — Real-World Project
Week 10–12 · 20 hours
Learning objectives
  • Project brief. Design a cluster-based CCS project for an assigned industrial region in India.
  • Mentored execution. Weekly reviews with domain mentors; modelling tools and reference datasets.
  • Certification & showcase. Project defence before an expert panel; verifiable certification on completion.
