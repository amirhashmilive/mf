Digital Education Initiative

EduSuTo (शिक्षा कल के लिए) —
Solar-Powered Offline Classrooms

Bringing digital-age pedagogy to off-grid tribal villages. Pre-loaded interactive tablets, solar power, and state board aligned interactive learning modules.

EdTech Architecture

Technology Bridging the Rural Divide

Engineered specifically for remote villages with no grid power or cellular coverage.

Solar Hub Power Stations

Self-contained 300W solar micro-stations providing clean electricity to charge tablet arrays daily even in dense forested terrain.

CGBSE Aligned Content

Complete curriculum for Classes 1–12 translated and contextualized with local examples in Hindi and Chhattisgarhi dialect.

Barefoot Digital Mentors

Educated village youth trained as community tutors who manage the tablets, facilitate lessons, and track child learning metrics.

Resilience

Zero Internet Dependency

All textbooks, animated science experiments, and math drills run natively on internal tablet storage. Syncing occurs via quarterly offline USB drives.

Attributable Gains

40% Literacy Improvement

Annual standardized assessments show EduSuTo students achieve 40% higher numeracy and reading scores compared to regional district baselines.
