Empowering 12,000+ women farmers across 12 districts of Chhattisgarh through SHGs, FPOs, livestock training, and grassroots governance.

Livestock

FPOs

Women-led FPOs across input, processing, market.

Subsistence to enterprise. Farmours moves women from being individual subsistence farmers to leaders of registered FPOs handling input, processing, and market. All training and materials are free for rural participants.
All courses free for rural Chhattisgarh communities. Certification on completion.
