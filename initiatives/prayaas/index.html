Prayaas (प्रयास) —
No Child Left Behind in Rural Schools

Free after-school remedial learning centers ensuring first-generation schoolgoers master foundational reading, writing, and arithmetic in a joyful, supportive environment.

Remedial Framework

Building Strong Foundations

Targeted support for children struggling in crowded government schools.

Foundational Literacy (FLN)

Activity-based learning modules ensuring every child can read age-appropriate text fluently by Class 3.

Basic Numeracy Drills

Contextual math games using pebbles, seeds, and local marketplace math to build intuitive arithmetic comprehension.

Dropout Prevention

Tracking student attendance and conducting home counseling for families at risk of pulling children out for seasonal agricultural labor.

Inclusive

First-Generation Empathy

Most Prayaas students have illiterate parents who cannot assist with homework. Our village tutors fill this critical gap with patient, nurturing mentorship.

Outcomes

92% Retention Rate

Prayaas centers achieve a 92% retention rate through secondary school, preventing early child labor and early child marriage.
