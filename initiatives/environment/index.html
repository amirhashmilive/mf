01 · Overview

The environment practice area

Rivers are the arteries of rural Chhattisgarh. When they silt up, dry up, or fill with sewage, the communities along their banks lose their drinking water, their irrigation, and their identity. Bolti Nadi — 'the speaking river' — was founded in 2016 to make those rivers heard.

Our mission

Make rural Chhattisgarh drought-free. Restore 8 priority rivers. Train communities to monitor their own water — without depending on external experts.

Our approach

Walk the river. Listen to villagers. Document with rigour. Publish community-authored reports. Pressure authorities to act on evidence.

Our impact

90 km Sakri River walked. 12+ villages restored. Check dams, STP, plantation triggered. 8 rivers under ongoing community-led monitoring.

02 · Two initiatives

Two initiatives, one landscape

The environment practice area runs through two initiatives: Bolti Nadi restores rivers and water security; the Social Heritage Walk preserves the cultural memory that lives along those rivers — temples, step-wells, tribal sacred groves, oral histories.
