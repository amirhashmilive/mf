Ecological Pillars

Integrated Environmental Action

Targeted interventions designed to counteract soil erosion, climate stress, and water depletion.

Bolti Nadi Campaign

Our flagship river basin conservation movement. Rejuvenating dying tributaries through participatory desilting and water table recharging.

Explore Bolti Nadi →

Native Riparian Buffers

Planting deep-rooted indigenous species (Arjun, Jamun, Karanj, Mahua) along riverbanks to curb erosion and regulate river water temperatures.

Discover Agro-Forestry →

Sacred Grove Protection

Safeguarding ancient tribal 'Devgudis' and sacred groves that act as micro-climate cooling zones and preserve rare botanical herbs.

Landmark Milestone

Expedition Sakri (140 km On Foot)

In 2018, Meer Foundation led an unbroken 140 km padayatra along the dried-up Sakri River basin, surveying every choke point and inspiring over 25,000 villagers to sign community covenants for water preservation.
