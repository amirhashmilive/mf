01 · Overview

The livelihood practice area

Rural Chhattisgarh's economy runs on smallholder farming — but for decades, women did the work and men held the assets. The livelihood practice area flips that script. By forming SHGs, training women in organic agriculture, and incorporating women-led FPOs, we move women from unpaid labour to recognised enterprise.

Our mission

Move 1 lakh+ rural households from subsistence to enterprise. Empower 12,000+ women farmers through direct market access and collective organisation.

Our approach

Start with savings groups. Layer on training. Aggregate through FPOs. Lock in 100% women-led governance at every level — base, federation, board.

Our impact

1L+ households reached. 860+ SHGs and 35 federations. 3,320+ households crossed ₹2.5L annual income in 2024-25. 18 registered women-led FPOs.

02 · Initiative

Farmours — women-led enterprise

Farmours is Meer Foundation's flagship livelihood initiative. Launched in 2020, it empowers 12,000+ women farmers through direct market access, women-led FPO formation, organic farming training, and 100% women-led governance.

03 · 5 strategies

Five strategies, one ladder

Each strategy builds on the one before. SHGs create trust. Agriculture training creates skills. Livestock creates daily income. FPOs create market power. Governance locks it all in women's hands.

01

Self-Help Groups

10-20 women per group. Weekly savings, internal lending, and peer accountability. SHGs are the base unit of the Farmours model — every other strategy flows from this.

860+ SHGs
35 federations
  • Weekly savings
  • Internal low-interest lending
  • Peer-guaranteed loans
  • 35 block-level federations
02

Agriculture & organic farming

Native seed preservation, composting, bio-pesticide preparation, SRI cultivation, crop rotation. Every training is hands-on, in the field, in the local language.

12,000+ women
trained
  • Native seed banks
  • Organic input training
  • SRI / SSI cultivation
  • Crop-rotation planning
03

Livestock & allied income

Goatery, poultry, dairy, and fishery. Women typically own and manage these assets, creating a daily income stream separate from seasonal crop sales.

4 streams
of income
  • Goatery modules
  • Backyard poultry
  • Dairy co-operatives
  • Fishery in restored rivers
04

Farmer Producer Organisations

FPOs aggregate produce, negotiate with buyers, and run processing units. They move women from price-takers to price-makers — from subsistence to enterprise.

18 FPOs
registered
  • Aggregate produce
  • Direct mandi access
  • Processing units
  • 7-day member payout
05

Governance & leadership

Every SHG, federation, and FPO has a women-led board. Training covers bookkeeping, AGM procedure, conflict resolution, and government scheme navigation.

100% women-led
boards

  • AGM procedure
  • Scheme navigation

Native seeds · organic inputs · fair markets. Farmours does not import technology. We revive what tribal Chhattisgarh already knew — native seed varieties, organic composting, and direct buyer-seller relationships — and scale it through SHG discipline.
