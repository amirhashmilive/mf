Agrarian Architecture

Three Pillars of Rural Enterprise

Building self-reliant farming communities through indigenous knowledge and modern market linkage.

Women SHG Collectives

860+ registered self-help groups providing micro-credit, bulk input purchasing, and collective bargaining power to women farmers.

Heirloom Seed Banks

Conserving 100+ indigenous crop varieties (millets, pulses, oilseeds) threatened by monoculture. Community-managed seed libraries ensure food sovereignty.

Bio-Composting & Market Access

Training farmers in Jeevamrit and Beejamrit bio-composting, eliminating chemical fertilizer costs, and connecting farmer produce to urban organic markets.

Economic Impact

Breaking the Debt Cycle

Before Farmours intervention, 78% of target farmers relied on moneylender credit. After SHG formation, internal lending replaces moneylenders at 0% interest, saving an average ₹12,000 per family annually.

CSR Alignment

Schedule VII Compliant

Farmours livelihoods qualify under CSR Item (ii) — promoting education & vocational skills, and Item (x) — rural development projects. Full CA utilization certificates provided.
