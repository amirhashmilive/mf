Core Program Portfolios

Four Key Sectors,
One Verifiable Mission

We combine indigenous knowledge with modern ecological and pedagogical science. Our integrated programs address water, schooling, public healthcare, and agrarian self-reliance across 12 districts in Chhattisgarh.

Program Architecture

Grassroots Programs in Action

Click on any sector to discover real methodologies, district case studies, and measurable outcomes.

Environment

Bolti Nadi (Speaking River)

Community-led rejuvenation of dying river tributaries, traditional check dam construction, desilting, and participatory hydrological mapping across the Mahanadi and Godavari basins.

Explore Bolti Nadi →
Education

Prayaas & EduSuTo

Remedial learning centers for first-generation rural and tribal learners, solar-powered digital tablets, curriculum alignment with state board standards, and foundational literacy.

Explore Education →
Health

Yoga Kutumb

Preventive holistic healthcare, daily village yoga sanghams, maternal and infant wellness, adolescent girl nutrition drives, and integration of traditional Ayurvedic principles.

Explore Yoga Kutumb →
Livelihood

Farmours

Sustainable rural livelihoods, indigenous climate-resilient heirloom seed conservation, bio-composting, and 860+ women farmer self-help groups breaking debt cycles.

Explore Farmours →

Social Heritage Walk
