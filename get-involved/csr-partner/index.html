Overview

Betterment Verification — not compliance theatre

Most CSR reports answer the question: 'Was the rule followed?' We answer a different question: 'Did lives measurably improve?' A programme can be fully compliant and produce no betterment. We treat betterment as the higher bar — and our research arm has audited 28 CSR programmes across 8 districts of tribal Chhattisgarh to prove it. When you partner with us, you receive Betterment Verification: proof, not press releases.

Partnership lifecycle

Four phases from concept to closure

From the first concept note to the final Betterment Verification report — here is what to expect at each phase of a CSR partnership.

1

Concept note

Share your CSR focus areas, budget envelope, and geographic interest. We co-design a programme that fits both your company's CSR policy and the Five Pillars Framework.

2

MoU & documentation

Formal MoU signed. CSR-1, 12A, 80G, and NGO Darpan documentation shared with your finance and legal teams. Payment milestones and reporting cadence agreed.

3

Implementation

Quarterly milestones. Field visits welcome — indeed encouraged. Mid-year review with your CSR team. Independent audit at closure by a CA empanelled with the CAG.

4

Impact report

Five-Pillars-Framework-aligned closure report. Betterment Verification — proof that lives measurably improved, not just compliance metrics. Raw survey data annexed.

Co-designed, not off-the-shelf

Frequently asked questions

We will not propose a duration shorter than what the work genuinely requires — and we will not stretch a duration to inflate the budget.
