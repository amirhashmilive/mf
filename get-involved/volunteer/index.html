Overview

Skills wanted — presence required

Volunteering at Meer Foundation is not tokenism. It is sustained, skilled contribution alongside our programme teams — in Dhamtari, Kanker, Bastar, and Balod districts, or remotely from anywhere in the world. Three months is the floor; many volunteers stay a year or more. We sign off only on real work shipped to real communities.

Open roles

Six roles, six skill sets

Each role card specifies the location, weekly commitment, and the programme you would join. Click apply on any role — the form below lets you select your role of interest.

Teaching mentor

Mentor Prayaas students in CGBSE Class 1–12 subjects — Hindi, English, Mathematics, Environmental Science, Science, Social Science, Sanskrit, Computer Science. Lesson plans and materials provided.

Field or remote 4 hr/week

River field researcher

Join Bolti Nadi river walks. Help document water quality, riparian ecology, and community dependence. Submit field reports. Walk the river — do not write about it from a desk in Raipur.

Field, Chhattisgarh Field expeditions

Photo / video volunteer

Document field programmes — yoga sessions, SHG meetings, river walks, learning centres. Build the media gallery archive. Own equipment preferred; we cover travel and food.

Field 2 field visits/month

Tech & design

Web, design, content, illustration. Help build digital tools for rural education — Prayaas online platform, Farmours FPO app, Bolti Nadi citizen-science forms. Stack: Next.js, Tailwind, Postgres.

Remote 8 hr/week

Field Notes writer

Write blog posts from field visits. Translate between Chhattisgarhi, Hindi, and English. Tell the story of a village, a woman, a river — in the language the subject would use.

Hybrid 2 posts/month

Yoga instructor

Lead free weekly yoga sessions in rural villages. Certification required (200-hour YAI/Yoga Alliance or equivalent). Sessions are part of Yoga Kutumb — our community wellness initiative.

Field 1 session/week
Application process

Four steps from form to field

From the moment you submit your form to the day you receive your certificate — here is what to expect. Total time from application to placement: typically 7–10 working days.

1

Apply online

Fill the form below. Tell us your skills, your city, and your time availability. No cover letter — just the form.

2

Phone screening

A 30-minute call with our volunteer coordinator. We discuss programme fit, your expectations, and ours.

3

Onboarding

Orientation session — online or at our Dhamtari office. Code of conduct signed. Programme team introduction.

4

Placement & mentoring

Assigned to a programme team. Quarterly reviews. Mentor support. Certificate on successful completion of 3+ months.

Mentor support

Each volunteer is paired with a programme mentor for the duration of their engagement.

Quarterly reviews

Structured feedback at the 3-, 6-, and 12-month marks. We invest in your growth.

Real deliverables

No fetching coffee. Your output ships to real communities. Your certificate cites specific work.
