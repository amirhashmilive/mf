Frequently asked questions

After your donation, you will receive an 80G receipt by email within minutes. The receipt carries your name, PAN, donation amount, date, and our 80G registration ID (AACAM4289JF20241). When filing your Income Tax return (ITR), enter the receipt details under Section 80G. The deduction is 50% of the donated amount, subject to the qualifying-limit rule (10% of your adjusted gross total income).
