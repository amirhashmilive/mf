Sequential Methodology

The AEIOM Operational Cycle

Every Meer Foundation initiative follows this battle-tested five-stage development pathway.

A

Awareness (चेतना)

Stage 01

Before soil is dug or seeds are sown, the community must articulate its own challenges. We mobilize Gram Sabhas, community radio broadcasts, street plays, and river padayatras to build collective consciousness regarding water security, public health, and child literacy.

✦ Community Dialectic ✦ Radio & Folk Media ✦ Gram Sabha Resolutions
E

Education (शिक्षा)

Stage 02

Knowledge translates inspiration into actionable capability. Through our Prayaas remedial learning centers, EduSuTo digital modules, and farmer hydrology workshops, villagers gain practical literacy in watershed management, digital tools, and preventative healthcare.

✦ Prayaas & EduSuTo Hubs ✦ Watershed Agronomy ✦ Maternal Health Modules
I

Intervention (हस्तक्षेप)

Stage 03

Direct field execution carried out collaboratively with local labour. This includes de-silting dying tributaries under Bolti Nadi, building percolation check-dams, establishing organic seed banks with Farmours, and conducting medical screening camps with Yoga Kutumb.

✦ 350+ Waterbodies Revived ✦ Check Dam Construction ✦ Direct Citizen Shramdaan
O

Ownership (स्वामित्व)

Stage 04

The critical juncture where NGOs often fail. Meer Foundation hands over full maintenance, financial stewardship, and operational governance to village-level Water User Committees (Pani Panchayats) and Women Self-Help Groups, preventing project abandonment.

✦ 860+ Self-Help Groups ✦ Village Maintenance Funds ✦ Women Leadership
M

Monitoring (निगरानी)

Stage 05

Longitudinal scientific tracking. Water tables are measured quarterly via piezometric wells; student learning outcomes are benchmarked annually against CGBSE standards; and academic papers are published in IJMEER to open-source all hydrological learnings.

✦ Quarterly Water Table Audits ✦ IJMEER Research Publishing ✦ Third-Party Auditing

How AEIOM Meets Global ESG & SDG Mandates

Corporate CSR leaders require defensible, auditable metrics for SEBI BRSR and international ESG reporting. The AEIOM architecture maps directly into key United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

SDG 6: Clean Water

Bolti Nadi river desilting and water table replenishment across 350+ rural ponds.

SDG 4: Quality Education

Prayaas remedial coaching and digital labs serving 15,000+ marginalized students.

SDG 5 & 8: Decent Work

860+ Self-Help Groups and Farmours farmer collectives creating women-led micro-enterprises.
