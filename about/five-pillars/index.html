01 · Overview

A communication framework for institutional communication

The Five Pillars Framework is the empirical distillation of fourteen years of field work across rural Chhattisgarh. It is not a preference. It is what the Betterment Question requires.

The Betterment Question

How does an institution prove — to a skeptical observer — that its presence has measurably improved the communities it serves? This is the founding question of FPF.

Five pillars, one architecture

Accountability, Execution, Initiative, Outcome, Meaning. Each pillar is necessary; none is sufficient. Together they form the architecture for answering the Betterment Question.

Pillar Coherence

The strength of the strongest pillar cannot compensate for the weakness of the weakest. Coherence, not individual pillar strength, is the architecture's core principle.

“Activity is not outcome. The architecture must demonstrate that communities are measurably better because of the intervention — not by the institution's own declaration, but with evidence that would convince a skeptical observer.”

— Amir Hashmi, on FPF Pillar O — Outcome

02 · The 5 Pillars

A · E · I · O · M — five pillars, one architecture

Each pillar answers a specific question. Together, the five answers constitute the architecture for institutional communication.

A
A— Accountability

Being answerable for outcomes, not just activities.

Institutions must specify, in advance, the outcomes they are answerable for — and build the architecture to track them. Accountability demands outcome, not activity.

Asks
What are we answerable for — and to whom?
E
E— Execution

Operational architecture that converts intent into implemented reality.

Delivery systems must actually reach the communities they serve. Capacity without reach is intention without effect. Execution produces what Initiative identifies.

Asks
Does our delivery system actually reach the last household?
I
I— Initiative

Originating from participatory engagement with communities, not imposed intent.

Programmes must be designed through participatory engagement with communities — not imposed from above. Communities must recognise the initiative as their own.

Asks
Did the community help design this — or did we impose it?
O
O— Outcome

Actual, demonstrable, attributable change — not activity or output.

Activity is not outcome. Output is not outcome. The architecture must demonstrate that communities are measurably better because of the intervention.

Asks
Are communities measurably better because of us?
M
M— Meaning

Communication that corresponds to reality and is usable by stakeholders.

Communication must be legible, verifiable, and actionable by the people it serves — not by the institution alone. Meaning makes outcome verifiable.

Asks
Is our communication legible, verifiable, and actionable?

Pillar Coherence — the core principle

An institution excellent at Outcome (O) but failing at Accountability (A) is not legitimate — it is dangerous. Coherence requires all five pillars to be present and demonstrable simultaneously. The strength of the strongest pillar cannot compensate for the weakness of the weakest.

SDG 5 · Gender Equality

SHG federations and women-led FPOs specify, in advance, the outcomes they are answerable for — and build the architecture to track them. Accountability is the architecture of agency.

Coherence in practice. A women-led FPO (Livelihood → E) that achieves commercial success but cannot show measurable community agency (Women → A) is incomplete. A river restoration (Environment → O) whose outcomes are not communicated legibly to the affected villages (Health → M) is incomplete. The framework catches what single-pillar metrics miss.

04 · FPF vs Other Frameworks

How FPF completes GRI, SDG, ESG, BRSR

FPF is a communication framework — it does not replace existing frameworks, it completes them. Each existing framework addresses part of how betterment is communicated. FPF adds the Pillar Coherence test that turns disclosure into demonstrable betterment.

GRI

Global Reporting Initiative

Corporate sustainability reporting
What it does

Discloses material impacts, indicators, and management approach across environmental, social, and governance dimensions.

What FPF adds

FPF adds the institutional-legitimacy test: activity (reported under GRI) is not outcome (required by FPF Pillar O). FPF completes GRI by demanding demonstrable community-level betterment.

SDG

UN Sustainable Development Goals

Global development agenda (2015-2030)
What it does

17 goals, 169 targets. Provides shared language for development priorities — but offers no architecture for institutional communication.

What FPF adds

FPF maps every Meer Foundation initiative to specific SDGs, then adds the Pillar Coherence test: progress on one SDG cannot compensate for failure on another. FPF completes SDGs by enforcing interdependence.

ESG

Environmental, Social, Governance

Investor-grade risk assessment
What it does

Investor lens on non-financial risk. Quantifies exposure but does not require demonstrable community-level outcomes.

What FPF adds

FPF reframes 'S' (Social) around the Betterment Question: not what risk the community poses to the firm, but whether the firm measurably improves the community. FPF completes ESG by inverting the lens.

BRSR

Business Responsibility & Sustainability Report (SEBI)

Mandatory Indian corporate disclosure
What it does

SEBI-mandated ESG disclosure for the top 1,000 listed Indian companies. Principle-based reporting across 9 principles.

What FPF adds

BRSR's Principle 2 (community well-being) and Principle 8 (inclusive growth) lack an enforcement architecture. FPF provides one — the A-E-I-O-M coherence test — turning disclosure into demonstrable legitimacy.

The synthesis

An institution may report under GRI, align to SDGs, score well on ESG, and file its BRSR on time — and still fail the Betterment Question. FPF is the communication framework that closes the gap. Disclosure becomes legitimacy only when activity is verified as outcome, when delivery is verified as reach, and when communication is verified as community comprehension.
