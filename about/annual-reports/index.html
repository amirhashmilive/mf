Frequently Asked Questions

Details on auditing practices, CSR compliance, and donation receipts.

Our accounts are independently audited annually by registered Chartered Accountants in accordance with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) auditing standards and filed with the Registrar of Societies and Income Tax Department.
Over the past three audited fiscal years, an average of 89.2% of total expenditures was deployed directly to grassroots field initiatives (river restoration, school learning hubs, farmer seed banks), while administrative overhead remained strictly capped under 10.8%.
For all CSR grant agreements, Meer Foundation provides quarterly milestone progress statements, geo-tagged photographic evidence, and final Chartered Accountant certified Fund Utilization Certificates for Board and MCA compliance filings.
