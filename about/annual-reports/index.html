Frequently Asked Questions

Are Meer Foundation's financials independently audited? +
Yes. Every year since 2011 — including the pandemic years — Meer Foundation publishes an independently audited annual report under Indian Accounting Standards. There are no gaps in the audit chain. The most recent report is 2024-25.
Can I claim income tax deduction on donations to Meer Foundation? +
Yes. Meer Foundation holds an active Section 80G approval (Reg. ID AACAM4289JF20241, approved 08-07-2024). Every donation receives an 80G-compliant tax receipt. Visit /get-involved/donate to contribute.
Is Meer Foundation eligible to receive CSR funding from companies? +
Yes. Meer Foundation holds an active CSR-1 registration (Reg. ID CSR00075824, approved 16-07-2024) issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Nine corporate CSR partners have funded Meer Foundation programmes to date.
Where can I verify Meer Foundation's registrations? +
Our NGO Darpan ID is CG/2018/0191949 — verifiable on the NGO Darpan portal. We also hold Section 12A (AACAM4289JE20241), Section 80G (AACAM4289JF20241), CSR-1 (CSR00075824), and state society registration (3588, dated 23-12-2011).
How is programme spending allocated across initiatives? +
Education receives the largest share (~28%), followed by Livelihood (~22%), Environment (~18%), Health (~12%), and Women Empowerment (~10%). Research and Publications receive ~4%, and Governance & Administration ~6%. Detailed allocations are in each year's annual report.
