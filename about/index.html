Our Mission

To rejuvenate dying rivers, empower tribal and rural communities through contextual quality education, advance preventative healthcare, and restore ecological biodiversity by building self-sustaining community institutions.

Our Vision

A resilient, equitable Central India where living rivers flow year-round, rural families achieve dignifying agrarian prosperity, and youth lead community innovation with pride in their cultural and natural heritage.
