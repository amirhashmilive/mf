01 · Overview

Who we are

Meer Foundation was founded in 2011 in Dhamtari, Chhattisgarh. We work with — never for — rural communities, focusing on the most vulnerable regions. Our approach prioritises skill development within communities rather than imposing solutions.

Our mission

Build self-reliant rural communities across Chhattisgarh through education, environment, health, livelihood, and women empowerment.

Our approach

Recruit professionals with empathy. Prioritise skill development within communities. Instil agency, not dependency.

Our impact

1 lakh+ households across 12 districts. 860+ SHGs. 90 km river walked. 142 downloadable PDFs. 15 years of audited work.

02 · Founder

Amir Hashmi — architect of the Five Pillars

Amir Hashmi founded Meer Foundation with a single conviction: institutions must demonstrate, with evidence that would convince a skeptical observer, that communities are measurably better because of their presence.

AH
Amir Hashmi
Founder, Meer Foundation

"Working with people, never for them. The strength of the strongest pillar cannot compensate for the weakness of the weakest."

Amir Hashmi founded Meer Foundation in 2011 with a single conviction: institutions must be able to demonstrate, with evidence that would convince a skeptical observer, that the communities they touch are measurably better because of their presence.

His work across Chhattisgarh — from the 90-kilometre Sakri River Walk to the formation of 860+ women's Self-Help Groups — is the empirical foundation of the Five Pillars Framework, a communication framework for transparent, evidence-based institutional communication that he sets out in his book of the same name.

Under his leadership, Meer Foundation has reached over one lakh households across twelve districts of Chhattisgarh, with a focus on marginalised Dalit and Tribal communities. The foundation's work is consistently aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

Dr. Sayed Amir Mustafa Hashmi
03 · Milestones

15 years of verifiable betterment

Key milestones in Meer Foundation's journey from a 2011 registration to a 12-district, 1-lakh-household operation.

2011
Foundation established
Registered under Chhattisgarh Society Registration Act 1973 (44). Reg. No. 3588.
2016
Bolti Nadi launched
Save River Initiative established. Mission: make rural Chhattisgarh drought-free.
2019
Sakri River Walk + Tiranga World Record
90-km river walk. 15-km human chain in Raipur. Founder Amir Hashmi led both.
2020
Farmours partnership
Empowering 12,000+ women farmers through market access and FPO formation.
2024
CSR-1, 12A, 80G approved
All three compliance registrations secured. Eligible for CSR funding and 80G donations.
2026
IJMEER Journal + Prayaas Virtual School
Quarterly peer-reviewed journal launched. CGBSE Class 1-12 free materials rolled out.
