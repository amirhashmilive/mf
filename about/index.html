Who We Are

14+ Years of Grassroots Change

Meer Foundation is a registered non-profit organization established in 2011. Based in Dhamtari, we have spent over 14 years working at the grassroots level to empower marginalized communities across 10+ districts of Chhattisgarh.

Our approach is rooted in empathy and professional expertise — recruiting professionals with diverse skills who work within communities to instill agency and drive long-term social, economic, and ecological change.
