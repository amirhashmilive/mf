The Three Pillars of Our Work

How an institution proves to a skeptical observer that its presence has fundamentally uplifted the communities it serves.

The Betterment Question

Every intervention begins with an empirical question: will this program leave the community measurably more resilient and self-reliant when the project cycle ends?

  • Independent ground baseline audits
  • Zero reliance on top-down assumptions

Working With People

We work alongside rural families, never for them. Sustainable uplift happens when local youth, farmers, and women leaders co-design and own the solutions.

  • 12,000+ women farmers in collectives
  • Village youth appointed as river stewards

Verifiable Evidence

Transparency is not an afterthought; it is our operational discipline. All financial allocations and outcome indicators are published openly for public audit.

  • 100% audited annual reports published
  • Real-time AEIOM governance tracking
AH
Founder & Architect

Dr. Sayed Amir Mustafa Hashmi

Social Hydrologist, Author & Grassroots Organizer

Dr. Hashmi founded Meer Foundation on December 23, 2011. His work across rural Chhattisgarh spans community-led river revitalisation, tribal education access, and authoring the Five Pillars Framework — an institutional model for transparent social governance.

Five Pillars Author Sakri Walk Convenor
Ecological Milestone · 2019

The 90-Kilometre Sakri River Walk

In 2019, Dr. Hashmi led an on-foot hydrological survey across 90 km of the dying Sakri River. The findings mobilized thousands of villagers, resulting in community check dams, riparian tree planting, and waterbody desilting.

  • 90 km walked on foot surveying water quality and runoff
  • Catalyzed the Bolti Nadi movement across 47+ rivers
  • Triggered local check dam construction in 12 villages
Institutional Journey

Key Milestones (2011–2026)

Fourteen years of disciplined growth from a single grassroots society to a 12-district state-wide movement.

2011

Foundation Registered

Registered under Chhattisgarh Societies Act 1973 (Reg. No. 3588) on December 23, 2011, establishing our core mission of rural self-reliance.

2016

Bolti Nadi Launched

Initiated the Save River movement to map, protect, and revive dying freshwater tributaries in the Mahanadi river basin.

2020

Farmours & SHG Scale

Scaled women-led regenerative agriculture and organic farming federations, creating sustainable livelihoods for 12,000+ farmers.
