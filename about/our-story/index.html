Our Story & Founder

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between rural potential and modern opportunity in Chhattisgarh.

Who We Are

Meer Foundation is a registered non-profit organization (Reg. No. 3588, Chhattisgarh Society Registration Act 1973) established in December 2011. Based in Dhamtari, we have spent over 14 years working at the grassroots level to empower marginalized communities.

Our approach is rooted in empathy and professional expertise. We don't just impose solutions; we recruit professionals with diverse expertise who work within communities to instill agency and drive long-term social, psychological, and economic change.

The Betterment Question

"How does an institution prove — to a skeptical observer — that its presence has measurably improved the communities it serves?"

Our Approach

Working with people, never for them. This founding conviction requires all our programmes to be demonstrable and community-led.

Founder & President

Amir Hashmi

Founder & President, Meer Foundation

Amir Hashmi (formally Dr. Sayed Amir Mustafa Hashmi) is a visionary social entrepreneur, researcher, and author. His research focuses on CSR communication and community development in Chhattisgarh and Jharkhand.

He is the creator of the Five Pillars Framework (FPF), a proprietary framework used by Meer Foundation to bridge the gap between corporate social responsibility and genuine societal betterment.

The Journey

Milestones — not slogans — that shaped the foundation.

2011
Foundation
Meer Foundation registered in Dhamtari, Chhattisgarh, beginning work in rural education and health awareness.
2014
Swachh Bharat Integration
Launch of rural sanitation drives across 40+ villages, aligning with the national Swachh Bharat mission.
2019
Sakri River Walk
A 90-kilometre on-foot expedition along the Sakri River. The resulting report triggered riverbank plantation, check dams, desilting, and a sewage treatment plant across multiple villages.
2019
Meri Jaan Tiranga Hai
On August 11, 2019, orchestrated a 15-km human chain in Raipur with over 5,000 citizens to promote national integration and rural unity.
2021
Post-Pandemic Rebuilding
Launched the Farmours initiative to rebuild rural livelihoods, eventually scaling to 860+ women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs).
2024
CSR-1 Eligibility & EduSuTo
Secured CSR-1 registration, unlocking direct corporate partnerships. Launched EduSuTo certification programmes for sustainability professionals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who founded Meer Foundation, and when? +
Amir Hashmi — formally Dr. Sayed Amir Mustafa Hashmi — founded Meer Foundation in December 2011. It was registered on 23 December 2011 under the Chhattisgarh Society Registration Act 1973 (44), Reg. No. 3588.
Is this Meer Foundation affiliated with the Shah Rukh Khan Meer Foundation? +
No. This Meer Foundation was founded by Amir Hashmi in 2011 in Dhamtari, Chhattisgarh. It is an independent registered non-profit and is not affiliated with any other organisation of the same name.
What was the founding vision? +
Amir Hashmi's founding conviction was that institutions must be able to demonstrate, with evidence that would convince a skeptical observer, that the communities they touch are measurably better because of their presence. The Five Pillars Framework (A-E-I-O-M) is the architecture for that demonstration.
What is the Sakri River Walk? +
In 2019, Meer Foundation conducted a 90-kilometre on-foot expedition along the Sakri River. The expedition documented water quality, agricultural dependence, and ecosystem conditions, and the resulting report triggered riverbank plantation, check dams, desilting, and a sewage treatment plant across multiple villages.
How is the founder involved today? +
Amir Hashmi continues as Founder, providing strategic direction, leading the Five Pillars Framework's evolution, and serving as Course Director for EduSuTo certification programmes. The foundation's day-to-day operations are run by a field team across 12 districts of Chhattisgarh.
Where is Meer Foundation headquartered? +
The registered office is at House No 103, Housing Board Colony, Hatkeshar, Dhamtari, Chhattisgarh 493773. Field operations span 12 districts of Chhattisgarh. Reach the team at meercare@live.com or +91 98261 21177.
