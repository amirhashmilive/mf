Refund & Cancellation Policy

Meer Foundation is a non-profit society sustained by the trust of donors and the fees paid by students of our certification programmes. This policy sets out, transparently, when and how refunds are issued, and how cancellations are handled.

01 · Overview

A note on refunds

This policy applies to two kinds of payments made to Meer Foundation: voluntary donations and programme fees (primarily for the EduSuTo certification courses). The two have different refund frameworks, described below.

Last updated: July 2026

By making a donation or paying a programme fee to Meer Foundation, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Refund & Cancellation Policy. If you have any questions before paying, please contact meercare@live.com — we would rather answer a question than process an unwanted refund.

7-day refund window

Full, no-questions-asked refund within 7 days for both donations and programme fees. The clock starts from the moment your payment is confirmed.

Case-by-case after 7 days

Refund requests received after the 7-day window are reviewed individually. Where the funds have not yet been allocated to programme expenditure, we will usually refund.

Transparent & documented

Every refund is recorded in our books and reflected in our audited annual report. We never make refund decisions on the basis of who you are or who you know.

02 · Donation refunds

Refunding a donation

Donations are voluntary contributions. We respect that circumstances change and that mistakes happen. The framework below describes when and how we refund donations.

Within 7 days · Full refund

If you notify us within 7 days of the donation being confirmed, we will issue a full refund — no questions asked. You do not need to give a reason. Email meercare@live.com with the transaction ID and the donor name.

After 7 days · Case-by-case

Refund requests received after 7 days are reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Where the donation has already been allocated to a specific programme expenditure (e.g., distribution of school kits), we may not be able to refund. Where it has not yet been allocated, we will usually refund in full. In all cases, we will explain our reasoning transparently.

Special situations

  • Duplicate donations. If you accidentally donated twice (e.g., due to a network error), we will refund the duplicate regardless of the 7-day window — please email us with both transaction IDs.
  • Unauthorized donations. If a donation was made without your authorisation, please report it immediately. We will refund and cooperate with any investigation.
  • 80G certificates. If a refund is issued after an 80G certificate has been generated, the certificate is automatically cancelled and cannot be used for tax purposes.
  • Recurring donations. You can cancel a recurring donation at any time. The cancellation takes effect from the next billing cycle; donations already debited follow the standard donation refund policy.

04 · Cancellation process

How to request a refund or cancellation

We have made the refund process as simple as possible. Follow the steps below; we will guide you through the rest.

Step 1
Email us
Send an email to meercare@live.com with the subject line 'Refund Request' or 'Cancellation Request'. Include your full name, the transaction ID (from your donation receipt or course enrolment email), and the amount paid.
Step 2
Verification
Our team will verify the transaction against our records. This usually takes 1–2 business days. If we need additional information, we will reach out to you by email.
Step 3
Approval
Once verified, we will confirm approval (or explain why a refund cannot be issued) by email. The approval email will include the refund reference number and the expected credit date.
Step 4
Processing
The refund is initiated to your original payment method. If that is not possible (e.g., expired card), we will request NEFT/IMPS details from you.
Step 5
Confirmation
Once the refund is initiated, you will receive a final confirmation email with the bank reference number. If the refund does not reflect in your account within 14 business days, please reply to that email.

Information to include in your refund request

  • • Your full name (as it appeared on the payment).
  • • The transaction ID — found in your donation receipt or course enrolment email.
  • • The amount paid and the date of payment.
  • • The reason for the refund (optional, but helps us improve).
  • • For programme fees: the course name and cohort you are enrolled in.
  • • A contact phone number, in case we need to verify your identity.
