1. Charitable Contributions

All donations made to Meer Foundation are voluntary charitable contributions utilized strictly for educational, environmental, health, and livelihood development programs across rural communities in Chhattisgarh.

2. Accidental Duplicate Transactions & Refunds

If an accidental double-charge or unintended transaction occurs due to network or gateway glitch, please notify us within 7 calendar days of the transaction date at meercare@live.com with the payment transaction ID and bank confirmation screenshot.

Upon verification by our accounts team, duplicate amounts will be refunded back to the original source payment account within 7 to 10 business days. Once an official 80G tax receipt has been generated and filed with the Income Tax Department, refunds cannot be processed under applicable tax regulations.
