01 · Overview

A note on this disclaimer

Meer Foundation operates this website as a public-information resource for its programmes, beneficiaries, donors, and partners. The information on this site is provided in good faith, but it has limits — which this disclaimer sets out clearly.

Last updated: July 2026

This disclaimer applies to all content on this website — including text, images, PDFs, courses, blog posts, and impact statistics. By using this website you acknowledge that you have read and understood this disclaimer. It is a companion to our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

In an emergency. If you are currently experiencing a medical emergency, please stop reading this website and call your local emergency number immediately (108 in India, 112 from mobile). The health-emergency content on this site is not a substitute for emergency medical services.
02 · General information

What this site is — and isn't

The content on this website is published for general informational purposes. It describes our work, our programmes, and the impact we have measured — but it is not a comprehensive or authoritative source on any specific subject.

What this site is

A public-facing record of Meer Foundation's programmes, impact, research, and educational resources. A point of contact for donors, volunteers, partners, and beneficiaries.

What this site is not

A substitute for professional medical, legal, financial, or educational advice. An authoritative textbook on climate science, river ecology, or first aid. A crisis-intervention service.

Educational intent

Our awareness materials — First Aid guides, river-conservation primers, climate briefs — are designed to spark curiosity and prompt action. They are simplified for general readers and do not capture every edge case.

Programme descriptions

Descriptions of our initiatives reflect current practice at the time of writing. Programme design evolves with field learning; specific activities in your district may differ from what is described here.

05 · Professional advice

When to seek qualified help

Several of our initiatives — particularly Yoga Kutumb, Farmours, and EduSuTo — publish health, agricultural, or technical content. This section makes clear that none of that content is a substitute for advice from a qualified professional.

Medical advice disclaimer

The health-emergency guides published by our Yoga Kutumb initiative — Cardiac Emergency, Stroke Awareness, Respiratory Distress, Trauma & Injuries, Diabetic Emergency, Allergic Reactions, and Mental Health Crisis — are awareness materials for the general public. They describe what to do while awaiting professional medical help. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always seek the advice of a qualified medical practitioner with any questions you may have about a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read on this website. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (108 in India, 112 from mobile) immediately.

Legal advice

References to laws, regulations, and statutory provisions on this website — including in our compliance disclosures and legal pages — are general informational summaries. They are not legal advice. For advice on a specific legal situation, please consult a qualified advocate.

Agricultural advice

Farmours' sustainable-agriculture primers are written for the agro-climatic context of Chhattisgarh. Soil, water, and pest conditions vary widely; what works in Dhamtari may not work in Bastar. Please consult your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra or our field team before applying any technique at scale.
