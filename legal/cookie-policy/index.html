02 · What cookies are

A short primer

If you have ever wondered what a cookie actually is — and why every website seems to ask about them — this section is for you.

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store on your device. When you revisit the website, your browser sends the cookie back. This lets the website “remember” things — for example, that you have logged in, or that you prefer dark mode.

Cookies are not programs. They cannot run code, install software, or access other files on your device. They contain only the information the website itself placed in them — typically a short identifier and a few preference flags.

There are three broad categories:

  • Essential — required for the website to function. Without them, login, forms, and security features break.
  • Preference — remember your choices (theme, language, consent) so you don't have to set them again.
  • Analytics — measure traffic and usage patterns in aggregate so we can improve the site.
What we do NOT use. We do not use advertising cookies, social-media tracking pixels, retargeting cookies, or cross-site tracking cookies. Our commitment is to a calm, privacy-respecting web experience.

04 · Managing preferences

How to control cookies

You are in control. Every modern browser lets you accept, reject, or selectively allow cookies. The instructions below cover the most common browsers.

In your browser

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.
  • Brave: Settings → Shields → Cookies.

For analytics

If you prefer to opt out of Google Analytics across all websites that use it, Google provides a browser add-on:

Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on

For Indian users, the Web Analytics Opt-out is also honoured by our Google Analytics configuration — your visit will not be recorded.

What happens if I disable all cookies?

The public-facing website — homepage, programme pages, blog, contact form — will continue to work fully. Only logged-in functionality (EduSuTo course progress, Prayaas Online) requires essential cookies. Without the preference cookie, your theme choice will not persist across visits. Without the analytics cookie, we will simply not see your visit in our aggregate traffic reports.
