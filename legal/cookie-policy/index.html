Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal data more broadly. Where this Cookie Policy refers to "cookies", it includes similar tracking technologies such as local storage and web beacons.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

2. Cookies We Use

We use cookies minimally — only for essential functionality, anonymised analytics, and remembering your preferences. We never use advertising cookies.

Name Type Purpose Duration
_ga, _gid Analytics (Google) Used to distinguish users and calculate visitor, session and campaign data for the site's analytics reports. IP addresses are anonymised. 2 years / 24 hours
theme Preference (Local) Remembers your preference for light or dark mode. Persistent
session_id Essential (Local) Maintains your logged-in state if you are accessing EduSuTo or Prayaas Online portals. Session

3. Managing Your Preferences

You can control and manage cookies in various ways. Most browsers allow you to view, manage, delete and block cookies for a website.

  • You can block cookies by activating the setting on your browser that allows you to refuse the setting of all or some cookies.
  • If you use your browser settings to block all cookies (including essential cookies) you may not be able to access parts of our site (e.g., logged-in areas).

If you prefer to opt out of Google Analytics across all websites that use it, Google provides a browser add-on: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, which pages are read, how visitors find us, and what devices they use. IP addresses are anonymised before processing. Google's privacy policy applies to data they collect; our configuration prevents cross-site tracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you use cookies for advertising? +
No. We do not run any advertising on this website and do not use any advertising-cookie networks (Google Ads, Meta Pixel, etc.). We never have, and we never will.
Can I browse the site with all cookies disabled? +
Yes. Essential cookies are required only for logged-in functionality (course enrolment, Prayaas Online). The public website — homepage, programme pages, blog — works fully with all cookies disabled.
What is the difference between first-party and third-party cookies? +
First-party cookies are set by the website you are visiting (meerfoundation.co.in). Third-party cookies are set by a different domain — typically an embedded service like Google Analytics. We use only one third-party cookie service: Google Analytics, configured for anonymised IP collection.
Do you use cookies to track me across other websites? +
No. We do not engage in cross-site tracking. Our Google Analytics configuration does not share data with third-party advertising networks.
How often do you review this policy? +
Annually, or sooner if our cookie use changes materially. The last review was in July 2026.
