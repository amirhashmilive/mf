Press FAQs

How do I request an interview with the founder? +
Write to meercare@live.com with the outlet, angle, and deadline. The founder is available for interview on 48 hours' notice. Phone interviews can often be arranged faster.
Can I visit a project site? +
Yes. Site visits can be arranged across eight Chhattisgarh districts with 48 hours' notice. Write to us with the site you'd like to visit and preferred dates. We handle logistics.
Can I use gallery photographs in my article? +
Yes — every photograph in our Media Gallery is CC-BY 4.0. Use it freely for editorial purposes with attribution to Meer Foundation, Dhamtari.
Do you charge for press access? +
No. Press access — interviews, site visits, embargoed briefings — is always free for working journalists. We do not accept payment for placement.
How do I get on the press release distribution list? +
Write to meercare@live.com with 'Press list — add' in the subject line, and your outlet and contact details in the body.
