Press desk · 1 day SLA

Why a press desk? Because rural Chhattisgarh is reported on from the outside — by journalists who fly in, file a story, and fly out. Our press desk exists to give journalists direct, fast, accurate access to the people doing the work, so the reporting reflects what is actually happening on the ground.

05 · Awareness

Why rural news desks matter

Mainstream media under-reports rural India. When rural Chhattisgarh does appear, it is usually as crisis — drought, conflict, distress migration. Our press desk exists to surface the everyday work of rural communities, in their own words, on the record.
