01 · Overview

Radio for the last mile

Meer FM 90.4 was launched to reach the villages that television forgets and the internet cannot yet serve. Where literacy is uneven and smartphones are shared between families, the radio remains the most reliable, most democratic, and most trusted medium in rural Chhattisgarh.

Why radio?

In villages where newspapers arrive a day late and television signals fade, the radio is on — in every home, in every field, in every tea stall. It is the medium of the last mile.

Community-owned

Every show is anchored by trained community reporters — village youth, SHG members, and educators. We don't broadcast at the village; we broadcast with it.

14 hours / week

Six weekly shows, two hours of original programming each weekday evening, plus weekend feature shows. All episodes archived as on-demand podcasts.

05 · Awareness

Why community radio still wins

In an age of streaming and feeds, FM radio remains the most resilient, most affordable, and most inclusive medium in rural India. It reaches every household regardless of literacy, income, or smartphone access.
