Field Notes FAQs

How do I subscribe to Field Notes? +
Add the RSS feed URL (meerfoundation.org/emerging/field-notes/rss.xml) to your newsreader (Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire, etc.). We do not run a newsletter and we do not store your email address.
Why don't you have a newsletter? +
Because RSS is better. It is open, decentralised, and does not require us to track your reading habits. Add the feed once, and every post arrives automatically. No inbox clutter.
Who writes the posts? +
Every post is written by the Meer Foundation team member who did the work — field staff, educators, researchers, and occasionally our founder. We do not ghost-write.
Can I republish a Field Notes post? +
Yes. Field Notes posts are licensed CC-BY 4.0. You may republish any post in full or in part, with attribution to Meer Foundation and a link back to the original post.
