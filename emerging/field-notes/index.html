01 · Overview

First-person field reporting

Field Notes is Meer Foundation's blog — first-person field reports, analysis, and reflections from our team across Chhattisgarh. Where the rest of the website describes what we do, Field Notes explains why we do it, what we are learning, and where we are failing.

Field-authored

Every post is written by the team that did the work — field staff, educators, researchers, and on occasion the founder. No ghost-writing, no PR filters.

Weekly cadence

One new post every week, published on Friday morning IST. Subscribe via RSS and it lands in your newsreader automatically.

Tag-filterable

Filter posts by programme — Bolti Nadi, Farmours, Prayaas, EduSuTo, Yoga Kutumb, Heritage, FPF, Research. Read what you care about.
