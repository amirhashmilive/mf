01 · Overview

A visual archive of rural Chhattisgarh

The Media Gallery is Meer Foundation's open visual archive — photographs and short films from every programme, every district, every year of our work. Use it for journalism, research, classroom teaching, or simply to see what rural Chhattisgarh looks like through the eyes of the people who live there.

Photographs

High-resolution photographs from every Meer Foundation programme. Each frame is captioned, dated, and geotagged to its district.

Short films

Three-to-eight-minute films on flagship initiatives — Sakri River Walk, FPO harvests, Prayaas classrooms, heritage walks, and Tiranga.

CC-BY 4.0

All media is free for editorial use with attribution to Meer Foundation, Dhamtari. Commercial use requires written permission.

03 · Awareness

Why visual archives matter

Rural India is photographed from the outside — by visiting journalists, by development tourists, by NGOs who treat villagers as subjects. This gallery is photographed from the inside, by community reporters who live in the frame.
