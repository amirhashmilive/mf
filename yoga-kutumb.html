Frequently Asked Questions

What is Yoga Kutumb?

Yoga Kutumb is a Meer Foundation initiative that promotes holistic health, yoga culture, and community-driven wellness. It is not a competition â€” it is a family (kutumb) of people who believe wellness should be accessible to all.

Who can join Yoga Kutumb?

Everyone. Yoga Kutumb is designed for every body, every age, every ability. Whether you are a student, a working professional, a senior citizen, or someone who has never tried yoga â€” you are welcome.

Is there a fee to participate?

Community yoga sessions and most resources are completely free. Certification programs have nominal fees to cover materials and platform costs, with the Quick Wellness Certification being entirely free.

What happens if I don't meet the required score on assessments?

There is no "fail" at Yoga Kutumb. If you do not meet the threshold, you can continue learning and retake the assessment after 24 hours. The goal is learning, not scoring.

How can my company partner with Yoga Kutumb?

Reach out to us through the Contact section. We will discuss your CSR goals, team size, and community reach. We then design a customised wellness program with measurable impact metrics.
